Modeling a 3 qubit Linear Optical Quantum Computer to identify and optimize sources of noise

I started working on this February 2026 when the Georgia Tech Quantum Computing Association began a new project in assembling a linear optical quantum computer on our own. Our goal is to make the most efficient system possible using the limited optical components we have access to given our budget, which means trying to figure out a way to identify potential sources of optical noise that might result from component configurations. Naturally, trying to model the entire setup in COMSOL is a worthwhile endeavor.
